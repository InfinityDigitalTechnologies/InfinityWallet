## 🔐 Security

Security and privacy are important when managing digital assets.

- Keep your recovery phrase in a secure offline location.
- Never share your private keys or recovery phrase.
- Always verify transaction details before confirming.
- Download application files only from sources you trust.

**Important:** Anyone who obtains your recovery phrase or private keys may gain control of your wallet and funds.
